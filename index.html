TurnOver beta

Download TurnOver 2.0 (build 22.4), a zip of about 21 MB, for macOS 14 or later, Apple silicon or Intel.

Unzip it, move TurnOver to your Applications folder and open it. TurnOver updates itself after that.

What's new: every build's changes, and which reported problems are fixed.

Read the beta guide: getting started, the keys, and how to send feedback. Also as plain text.

TurnOver is free and open source, made in spare time. If it helps you: sponsor on GitHub, monthly or once, or tip on Ko-fi, no account needed.

The guides: getting started, the TurnOver layer, reading and say all, the apps, keyboard styles, CoExist, and what to do if something goes wrong.

What's coming: the roadmap.
