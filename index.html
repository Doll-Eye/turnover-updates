TurnOver beta

Download TurnOver 1.0 (build 3), a zip of about 6 MB, for macOS 14 or later, Apple silicon or Intel.

Unzip it, move TurnOver to your Applications folder and open it. TurnOver updates itself after that.

What's new: every build's changes, and which reported problems are fixed.

Read the beta guide: getting started, the keys, and how to send feedback. Also as plain text.
