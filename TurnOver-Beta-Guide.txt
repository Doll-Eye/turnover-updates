TURNOVER 1.0 BETA 1: A GUIDE FOR TESTERS

TurnOver is a screen reader for the Mac, built in the shape of NVDA, that works alongside VoiceOver.
This beta supports three applications: Mail, Safari and Terminal, with Microsoft Word as an early preview
that you turn on if you want to try it (see MICROSOFT WORD below). When one of them comes to the front,
TurnOver switches VoiceOver off and takes over; when you leave it, VoiceOver comes back. Every other
application stays with VoiceOver. It runs on macOS 14 or later, on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

This is a proof of concept. Thank you for testing it. Please tell us what goes wrong (see SENDING
FEEDBACK below).


GETTING STARTED

1. Copy TurnOver to your Applications folder and open it. A welcome message explains the permissions.
2. macOS asks for two permissions. In System Settings, Privacy and Security, turn TurnOver on under
   Accessibility (called Device Control and Data Access on macOS 27), and under Input Monitoring.
3. Switch to Mail, Safari or Terminal. VoiceOver goes off, TurnOver says "TurnOver on", and reads.

TurnOver updates itself: once a day it checks for a new build, says "A TurnOver update is available", and
opens a window where you choose Install Update or Skip. TurnOver menu, Updates: check now, turn the daily
check off, or turn on "Download and install updates automatically" (the update then installs next time TurnOver
quits, or straight away from that menu). Your permissions carry over to each update. Updates, What's new, lists
every build's changes, including which reported problems are fixed.

TurnOver can open when you log in: TurnOver menu, Preferences, General, Open TurnOver at login. While it is
running it has an item in the menu bar, called TurnOver.


THE KEYS THAT MATTER

The TurnOver key is Caps Lock, or Control and Option together (VoiceOver's VO keys): hold either and press
another key. (Pressed twice on its own, Caps Lock still turns capitals on and off.) In the lists below,
"TurnOver" means Caps Lock or VO.

  TurnOver N            The TurnOver menu: preferences, feedback, quit. Up and Down move; Right or Return opens
                        a submenu or changes a setting; on a number (rate, volume) Left turns it down; elsewhere
                        Left goes back a level (Shift Up or Down steps a choice back); Escape closes the menu.
  TurnOver slash        Every shortcut that works where you are, grouped. Enter runs one.
  TurnOver V            Hands this application back to VoiceOver.
  Control            Stops speech.
  TurnOver 1            Input help: every key says what it does, without doing it. Press again to stop.
  TurnOver Tab          Says what has focus.
  TurnOver L            Reads the current line.
  TurnOver A            Reads from here to the end (say all).
  TurnOver Command C    Copies the last thing TurnOver said to the clipboard.
  TurnOver Q            Quits TurnOver.
  TurnOver Shift Escape Screen Curtain on or off (the screen goes black; TurnOver carries on as normal). VO Shift
                        Underscore also works. If VoiceOver's curtain is on, TurnOver keeps it on when it takes
                        over. To choose: TurnOver menu, Preferences, Speech, Screen Curtain while TurnOver leads.
  Command Control T      Starts TurnOver, or quits it, from anywhere. Quitting turns VoiceOver on. A
                         rising or a falling chime says which. It works even if TurnOver is stuck:
                         the way out that always works (a small helper, TurnOver Key, listens for it
                         from login; you will see it in System Settings, General, Login Items).

VoiceOver's commands. TurnOver uses VoiceOver's own commands (VO is Control and Option together), so
what you already know works. The TurnOver key is either Caps Lock or VO to start with; to keep only one:
TurnOver menu, Preferences, Keyboard, TurnOver key.
  VO M                       The application's menus. VO M twice: the status menus at the right.
  VO D                       The Dock.
  VO Shift M                 The context menu.
  VO F3                      Describes the item with focus.
  Right Option T             The date and time (VO F7 also works).
  VO F                       Find, in any application TurnOver leads: type the text and press Return.
                             TurnOver goes to the next match and leaves you there (a message in Mail's
                             list, a line in a page or a message, text in a document or Terminal).
                             Escape cancels. VO G and VO Shift G: the next and previous match.
  VO T                       The formatting at the caret (font, size, style).
  VO I                       The Item Chooser: everything in the window, or every line of a page or
                             message. Type to narrow the list; Backspace widens it; Enter goes there.
  VO U                       The rotor. Left and Right choose a kind: on a page or message, headings,
                             links, form controls, landmarks, tables; in a window, buttons, text
                             fields, tabs, lists and so on; in Word, headings and comments. Up and Down
                             move through that kind, typing narrows it, Enter goes there.
  VO Command H, L, J, T, X, G  Next heading, link, form control, table, list, graphic (add Shift: previous).
  VO Space                   Presses the item.
(A Windows layout, Alt for the menus and so on, is set aside for now; it will come back later.)

Moving through anything, even what the keyboard cannot reach (object navigation):
  TurnOver Shift Right / Left   Next or previous item in the window.
  TurnOver Shift Down / Up      Into an item, or out to what holds it.
  TurnOver Enter                Presses the item (or puts you in it to type).



SAFARI (AND MAIL MESSAGES): BROWSE MODE

Web pages and email messages are read as a document, like NVDA's browse mode.
  Up / Down arrows       Line by line.        Option arrows: word by word.
  H / Shift H            Next / previous heading. 1 to 6: headings of that level.
  K, B, E, F, L, T, D    Link, button, edit field, form field, list, table, landmark (Shift goes back).
  Enter                  Activates a link or button.
  TurnOver Space            Switches between browse mode and typing into the page (focus mode).
Video players (YouTube) switch to focus mode by themselves when you Tab into them or press Enter on Play, so
their own keys work: on YouTube, K plays and pauses, J and L skip back and forward. Escape goes back to
browse mode.
  Shift with arrows      Selects text; Command C copies it.
Back in Safari returns you to where you were on the previous page.
Find (Command F): Return reads the line of each match; Escape puts you on it in the page.
Safari's sidebar (Reading List, bookmarks) is not reached by Tab: TurnOver Shift Up to the window, TurnOver
Shift Down, then TurnOver Right to the list, and TurnOver Enter. Return opens an item; the context menu
(VO Shift M, or Right Option) has Delete.


MAIL

Up and Down move through the message list; Home and End go to the first and last message. The
message itself is read in browse mode (above). Settings windows: Up and Down move between the tabs
at the top, Tab goes into the page, Shift Tab comes back to the tab.


TERMINAL

New output is read as it arrives; your own typing is not read twice. After cd, the new folder is
said. Tab completions and the Up arrow's history are read.
  TurnOver Up / Down        Reads earlier lines without disturbing the shell; TurnOver Home / End: top, bottom.
  Delete, Option Delete  Says what was deleted.
  Left / Right           Reads the character at the cursor; Option Left / Right: word by word.
  TurnOver Command C        Copies the last output TurnOver read.
Not yet: full-screen programs such as less, man, vim and top.


MICROSOFT WORD (PREVIEW)

Word support is an early preview, off to start with, as it is still changing day to day. To try it: TurnOver
menu (Caps Lock N), Applications, Microsoft Word, set to "TurnOver leads". (Word is only listed when it is installed.) Your reports on Word are very welcome.

The document reads as you move, with page and line where Word gives them.
  TurnOver H / Shift H      Next / previous heading.
  TurnOver M / Shift M      Next / previous comment. TurnOver Option C reads the comment at the caret.
  TurnOver E / Shift E      Next / previous spelling error.
  TurnOver R / Shift R      Next / previous tracked change.
  TurnOver Page Down / Up   Next / previous page.
  TurnOver F7               The elements list: headings and comments.
  Control F2             Word's menus.
  Command B, I, U, E, L, R, J, Command Option 1 to 3 (headings), Command Shift N (Normal) say what changed.
With the VoiceOver keyboard style, where VO M is the menu bar and VO F7 the time:
  VO Command M / R / E   Next comment / tracked change / spelling error (add Shift for previous).
  VO Command H           Next heading.     VO U   The elements list.
Tables: arrowing into a table says its size, then the row, column and text; moving says what changed, with the
column's title (from the first row) and the row's title (from the first column). Empty cells say "blank".
  Tab / Shift Tab            Next / previous cell (Word selects it: "selected" means typing replaces it).
  TurnOver arrows            Cell to cell without selecting; "edge of table" at the edges.
The ribbon: F6 moves between the ribbon, sidebar, status bar and toolbar. On a ribbon tab (Home, Insert...), Left
and Right change tab; Down or Tab goes into its commands; Tab and Shift Tab move through them. Escape, from the
ribbon, sidebar, status bar or toolbar, goes back to the document.
If you end up in Word's sidebar, TurnOver menu, Microsoft Word, "Show or hide the sidebar" takes you back.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

If TurnOver stops responding, VoiceOver comes back by itself within a few seconds, and your keys work
again. If it is still stuck after that, TurnOver restarts itself.
TurnOver V always hands the application to VoiceOver. Quitting TurnOver (TurnOver Q, or the TurnOver menu, or
the menu bar item) leaves VoiceOver in charge everywhere.


SENDING FEEDBACK

TurnOver menu (TurnOver N), Send feedback:
  Save a report, leaving out what TurnOver said        (recommended)
  Save a report, including what TurnOver said          (helps with reading problems; may include the
                                                     text of your emails and pages)
A zip file is saved on your Desktop. Attach it to your message, and say in a sentence what you were
doing and what you expected.

TurnOver keeps its own logs for 30 days, on your Mac only:
~/Library/Application Support/TurnOver/logs/


REMOVING TURNOVER

TurnOver menu, Uninstall TurnOver, Uninstall now. It removes TurnOver's login items, puts the app, its
settings and its logs in the Bin, resets its Accessibility, Input Monitoring and Automation permissions, and
turns VoiceOver on. This is also the cleanest way to start again if permissions get muddled: uninstall, then
download TurnOver afresh.
