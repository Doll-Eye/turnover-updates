The TurnOver layer

TurnOver keeps its own commands behind one key, so it never takes a shortcut away from your apps: press Command Control Comma, hear "TurnOver", then press a letter. It works in every app, including ones VoiceOver reads.

The letters

LetterWhat it does
MThe TurnOver menu
SThe Settings window: every setting, sections down the side
TSwitches the app in front between TurnOver and VoiceOver, and remembers it (also straight away: Command Control Shift T)
DDirect: every key goes straight to the app in front and neither TurnOver nor VoiceOver says anything there, for a game or app that speaks for itself. Command shortcuts (Command Tab, Command Q) still work. D again to leave; remembered
AWho reads: TurnOver in its apps, TurnOver everywhere, or VoiceOver everywhere
VCopies your VoiceOver settings: voice, speed, pitch, verbosity
KGoes round the keyboard styles: VoiceOver keys, NVDA keys, JAWS keys
J"That was wrong": marks this moment for your next feedback report
HThis guide
IDescribes the picture under TurnOver's cursor
CCopies the last thing TurnOver said
GTrackpad gesture control on or off (experimental)
RScreen recognition: reads a window from the screen itself
SlashEvery shortcut that works where you are, in a list; Return runs one

Any other key says the list again. Escape, or three seconds with no letter, cancels.

Command Control Period

A single press starts or stops TurnOver from anywhere, even if TurnOver has stopped answering: a small helper, TurnOver Key, listens for it from login.

The TurnOver menu

"experimental support".

  • This app: who reads the app in front, and that app's own commands. Apps whose support is new say
  • Settings: speech (with the say all voice), keyboard, braille, documents, web pages, sounds, and Experimental.
  • Apps: which apps TurnOver reads.
  • Keys: every shortcut, keyboard help, and your own keys.
  • Send feedback, About and updates, Quit.

In the menu, Right or Return opens a submenu or changes a setting; on a number (rate, volume) Left turns it down. Settings that restart TurnOver change only with Return.
