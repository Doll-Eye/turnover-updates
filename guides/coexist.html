CoExist

CoExist keeps VoiceOver running alongside TurnOver. Wherever TurnOver reads, VoiceOver is silenced at the sound level, so you hear only TurnOver; in an app you give to VoiceOver, VoiceOver is heard at once, as if TurnOver weren't there. Switching is instant, and nothing is said as it happens. It is on unless you turn it off.

  • If TurnOver stops or gets stuck for more than a few seconds, VoiceOver is heard again.
  • Nothing of VoiceOver's is changed: its settings, voices and Activities stay as they are.
  • With CoExist on, TurnOver's own braille is off: VoiceOver keeps the display.

Turning it off

The TurnOver menu, Settings, Experimental, CoExist, then Return. TurnOver restarts and turns VoiceOver off where it reads, as it did before CoExist.

Good to know

Silencing VoiceOver's sound from outside is new, and a macOS or VoiceOver update could change how it behaves. Please report anything odd.
